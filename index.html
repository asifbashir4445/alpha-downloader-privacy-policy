ALPHA PULSE — PRIVACY POLICY

Alpha Develops · Last updated 9 November 2026

Alpha Pulse is a tool to extract audio from videos and to save videos and audio to your device from links you provide. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app has no account system and no login. We do not run our own analytics or trackers.

How extraction and saving work

When you choose a video or file already on your device, all processing happens entirely on your device.

For links to some online platforms, the link you paste is sent to our resolver server, which finds and returns the media address for that link. The media then downloads to your device. For a few platforms, the file passes through our relay server on its way to your phone. We do not store your media, and links are processed only to answer your request.

Clipboard

When you open the app, it may read your clipboard once to check whether you have copied a media link, so it can offer to extract or save that media for you. The clipboard content is used only for this purpose, in the moment, and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Permissions

Internet — used to fetch the media you choose to save, and to contact the resolver server described above.

Storage / Media — used only to save your extracted audio and saved videos to your device, and to read videos you pick from your device for extraction.

Notifications — used only to show download and extraction progress.

The Vault

Files you place in the Alpha Vault are encrypted on your device. The encryption keys never leave your device, and we cannot access your Vault contents.

Advertising

Alpha Pulse shows occasional ads provided by our advertising partner, Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). Its software (SDK), built into the app, may collect and process your device's advertising ID, device information (such as model and operating system), your approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about the ads you see and interact with. Unity handles this data under its own privacy policy: Unity — unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Ads appear only occasionally, at natural break points in the app. Your first download is always ad-free; after that, you will never see more than one ad every 2 minutes of using the app. Ads are never shown inside the Alpha Vault or the player, and there are no banner ads. A future Pro version of Alpha Pulse will remove ads.

Third-party content

If you choose to save media from a public link, that content belongs to its respective platform and owners. You are responsible for using saved content in line with those platforms' terms and applicable copyright law. The app is not affiliated with any social media platform.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Alpha Pulse (by Alpha Develops) does not keep your data on any server. The links you paste are processed only to answer your request and are not stored by us, so there is no server-side data of yours for us to hold or erase.

Data stored on your own device — your download history, settings, saved media, and Vault contents — stays under your control. To delete it: clear the app's storage in Android Settings → Apps → Alpha Pulse → Storage → Clear data, delete files from the in-app Library, or uninstall the app, which removes all of its on-device data.

The advertising ID used by Unity Ads can be reset or deleted at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Unity's handling of ad data is described in its privacy policy, linked above.

If you still wish to request deletion of any data connected with your use of Alpha Pulse, email asifbashir4445@gmail.com with the subject "Alpha Pulse — data deletion request". We will confirm what, if anything, exists and delete it. Because we do not store user data on our servers, in almost every case the answer is that there is nothing stored to delete. Requests are answered within 30 days.

Changes
