ALPHA DOWNLOADER — PRIVACY POLICY

Alpha Develops · Last updated November 2026

The short version

Alpha Downloader has no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising trackers. We cannot sell your information, because the app never collects it. Almost everything happens on your own phone.

What stays on your phone

Your downloads, your download history, your player positions and everything in the Alpha Vault are stored only on this device. The app has no cloud account and no copy of your files exists anywhere else.

When you paste a link

To find your video, the app has to visit the link you give it. For YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and X, your phone does this itself. For other platforms, the link is also sent to our own resolver computer, which asks the platform for the download links and sends the answer back to your phone. A few of the app's built-in tools ask small public helper services to read a link — tikwm.com, lovetik.com, api.fxtwitter.com, api.vxtwitter.com and ddinstagram.com. These services receive only the link you pasted — never your name or anything else about you, because the app does not have it. The video file itself then downloads straight from the platform to your phone; for a few blocked platforms (such as Telegram in some countries), our resolver computer carries the file for you.

What we never collect

No name, no email, no contacts, no location, no messages, no usage statistics. The app contains no analytics or advertising software at all. Its built-in diagnostics (About → Details) stay on your phone and are shown only to you.

The Alpha Vault

Vault files are encrypted on your device. Your Vault lock — PIN, password or pattern — is never stored; only a fingerprint of it is kept, just to check your entries. Fingerprint and face checks are performed by Android itself; the app never sees them. Vault files, the Vault's item list, and the lock's fingerprint are all excluded from Android backup, so they never leave your phone.

Your downloads

Please only download content you have the right to save, and respect the creators and the laws that apply to you.
